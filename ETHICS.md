# Ethics and consent documentation

This file records the ethics information that can be made public with the replication materials. Signed institutional approval documents are retained by the authors and do not need to be redistributed in the public GitHub repository unless the journal specifically requests public deposition.

## Classroom study

The classroom data collection received institutional ethics approval from:

- **Eszterházy Károly Catholic University (EKCU):** approval `RK/1058/2026`.
- **West University of Timisoara (WUT):** approval `UVT2026-071123/09.09.2026`.

The WUT approval is a favorable research-ethics opinion for the study titled *Notebook-Oriented Vibe Coding: Triple-Entry Software Engineering for AI-Assisted Computing Education*.

The author declaration records that classroom participation was voluntary and that respondents were informed before data collection that their responses would support research and scientific publication. It also records questionnaire submission as the act of consent.

**Submission check:** the current author-declaration materials also contain a generic “verbal informed consent” sentence. The manuscript and submission forms should use only the consent procedure that actually occurred. If consent was provided by voluntary questionnaire submission after the information notice, the conflicting “verbal informed consent” wording should be removed.

## Professional survey

The professional survey was anonymous and voluntary and instructed respondents not to provide confidential information, names, employer names, gender, or country. The manuscript also uses Q39 as a response-provenance filter.

**Status to confirm before submission:** the available project materials reviewed for this repository do not establish a separate ethics-approval number or a formally documented exemption/not-required determination for the professional survey. The manuscript must state the verified institutional status; it should not infer or invent an exemption.

## Practitioner interviews

The public interview corpus is anonymized and participant-reviewed; participants reviewed and approved the English texts before public archiving.

**Status to confirm before submission:** the available materials reviewed for this repository do not establish a separate institutional ethics-approval number or formal exemption/not-required determination for the practitioner interviews. The manuscript should report the actual status and the actual consent procedure.

## Public-release policy

The repository should publish approval **numbers and procedures**, not signed approval letters, unless the journal or an institution explicitly requires otherwise. The signed approvals should be retained by the authors and made available to the journal/editor on request.
